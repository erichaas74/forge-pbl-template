Simulation guide

Help and Rules

Your mission

{{ runtime.config.mission }}

Decision cycle

  1. Visit two shops and read their clues.
  2. Choose a route and note its travel cost.
  3. Buy two kinds of goods while keeping travel money.
  4. Predict sales and trip profit with the harder forecast.
  5. Travel, solve the trail math check, and explain both choices.
  6. Sell your goods, then finish and see your score.

Scoring

  • Trading: 40 points for profit toward the $50 goal.
  • Math: 40 points for the profit forecast and trail math.
  • Explain: 20 points for explaining both trail choices.

Your game score is separate from your teacher's review of your mathematics.

Words used in the game

Travel money: cash kept aside to pay for your route.

Money record: the list of money earned and spent.

Profit: money earned after subtracting all costs.

Saving

Meaningful actions save automatically on this device. The same project version resumes after a refresh.
