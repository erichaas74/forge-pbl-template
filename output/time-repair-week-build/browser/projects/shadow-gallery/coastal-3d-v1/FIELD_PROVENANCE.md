# Cultivated field

Original procedural geometry created by `docs/heist/build-coastal-field.mjs`. No external model or texture was used. The GLB has 12 meshes and 25,110 triangles; 1,817,892 bytes. Offline merging preserves the four separately selectable target groups.

Depicts maize plants with ears, sweet potato vines, harvested sweet potato roots, and an ear of maize for close inspection. Wheat is intentionally absent from the historical reconstruction. The model is illustrative: plot spacing, varieties, colours, leaf shapes and harvest arrangement are not an archaeological reconstruction of a specific excavated field. No ordinary potatoes are claimed for this scene.

Historical references:
- National Park Service, [Taíno Settlement](https://www.nps.gov/places/taino-settlement.htm), identifies maize and sweet potatoes among Taíno crops. Salt River Bay, St. Croix, provides regional comparative context, not a direct field record from Hispaniola.
- Florida Museum, [En Bas Saline material remains](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/histarch/research/haiti/en-bas-saline/material-remains/), provides archaeological maize and manioc evidence from Haiti. This page is not used to claim sweet potato remains from that site.

Students compare the scene with the painting and consult sources. Absence from a model alone is not historical proof. The model includes no answer popup announcing wheat's absence. Browser visual review remains pending because the computer-control runtime cannot initialize.
