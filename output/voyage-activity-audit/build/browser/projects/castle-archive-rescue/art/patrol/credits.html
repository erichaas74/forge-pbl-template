Animal art & animation

The animated fox is provided through the Khronos glTF Sample Assets collection.

  • Model: PixelMannen (2014), CC0 1.0.
  • Rigging and animation: tomkranis (2014), CC BY 4.0.
  • Conversion to glTF: @AsoboStudio and @scurest (2017), CC BY 4.0.

The model file is unmodified. The workshop scales and positions it, blends its Survey, Walk, and Run clips, and adds a path through the cage doorway. Castle scenery and mechanical animation are created for this workshop.
