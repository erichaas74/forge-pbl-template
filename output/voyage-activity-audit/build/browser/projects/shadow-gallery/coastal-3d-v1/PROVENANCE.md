# Canoe workshop

Original procedural model authored for shadow-gallery using `docs/heist/build-coastal-workshop.mjs`. No third-party mesh or texture was used. GLB generated with the installed Three.js GLTFExporter; original geometry and vertex colors are editable through the generator.

Illustrative reconstruction, not a scan or measured historical artifact. The model depicts a hollowed single-log hull, wooden rim, timber supports, loose wood chips and an unhafted stone implement on a work surface. Scale, hull profile, supports and tool shape are illustrative choices. Do not use those choices as archaeological evidence. Existing canoe-construction and canoe-tools source cards remain the historical references. No metal fasteners or intentional forgery details occur in this asset.

9 meshes; 26,768 triangles; 679,840-byte GLB. These are offline asset counts, not measured runtime performance. Browser visual review and classroom historical review remain pending. Animated people and interviews are not included.
