# Grade placements for the 16 existing projects

Reviewed 2026-09-14. These assignments apply to the current demo content, not every possible use of its reusable project type. They replace the broad catalog grade ranges with one primary placement.

## Sources and decision rules

The authority for grades 4–6 is the supplied [Forge framework CSV folder](./Forge_School_Master_Standards_Frameworks/), specifically the ELA, Math, Science, and Social Studies 4–6 exports. These are draft school standards. The separate [52-project map review](./FORGE_GRADES_4-6_PROJECT_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md) addresses proposed projects and is not this inventory.

Compared the 16 entries in `src/app/projects/project-catalog.ts`, their active packages/configuration, existing completed samples, and the eight-lesson evidence criteria in `src/app/projects/project-lesson-plans.json`. A related activity is a placement anchor, not proof of complete coverage of a bundled standard. Generic research or presentation skills do not move a historical unit into an otherwise unrelated grade's history sequence.

Grade 7–8 placements are provisional curriculum recommendations: no Forge grade 7–8 standards were supplied. Supporting references are [Tennessee seventh-grade mathematics](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/standards/math/Standards_Support_grade_7_Mathematics.pdf) and [Tennessee social studies standards](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/standards/ss/Social_Studies_Standards.pdf). Use the supplied Forge 4–6 sequence for this comparison even where state editions differ. These references do not substitute for a future Forge 7–8 crosswalk.

- Grade 4: **0** current projects assigned as their primary home.

- Grade 7: **5** — Time Repair, Cartographer’s Vault, Live Strategy League, Robot Delivery, Race Around the World.

No project was forced into grade 4 simply to balance counts. Several formats could support a future grade 4 version, but that requires a distinct content/assessment adaptation. The two unbuilt homepage concepts are excluded from these 16 assignments.
