# Continuing debate design — implementation decision

User direction recorded 2026-09-15 and explicitly applied to **both debate projects**.

The current design, eight-session sequence, screen placement, same-side critique, final performer rankings and persistence contracts are in the [Debate Studio build guide](../build/16_DEBATE_STUDIO_TEMPLATE.md).

## Decisions implemented

1. Hear the prompt, choose a side, form points and produce an opening in the first individual session.
2. Exchange arguments and submit the next response on group days.
3. Teach source/argument critique and produce a refined response on the following individual days.
4. Review another author's work on your own side; receive feedback on exact versions and link it to revisions.
5. Close with final exchanges and justified rankings of individual performers from both sides.
6. Keep the ongoing debate in the main viewport, with compact shared standards and side tasks above the disconnected tutor.
7. Keep manual writing in tutor-owned dialogs and preserve private context responses.
8. Keep all lessons and examples freely accessible during testing.
9. Use clearly labeled local file exchange until authenticated live delivery is connected.

Optional speech-model videos remain a resource-selection decision. [Student resource ideas](STUDENT_RESOURCES.md) provides a shortlist and concrete critique tasks. No placeholder video is presented as a finished resource.

The [earlier design discussion](archive/REPUBLIC_FLOW_DESIGN_BEFORE_IMPLEMENTATION.md) is preserved as history. Its unresolved wording and server-delivery proposals are superseded by the implementation decisions and explicit limits above.
