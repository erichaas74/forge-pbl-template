## Implemented interactions

Drag to circle the canoe, use the Walk left/right buttons for bounded 30-degree movement, look down into the hollowed interior, and move closer/back within limits. Select hull, rim or stone directly by raycast, or use equivalent keyboard-accessible target buttons. Selection changes the camera focus and exposes the actual 3D surface from another angle. Whole canoe resets the camera; Escape provides the same action. All operating controls are overlaid inside the scene. No question fields or interviews were added.

This is bounded orbit/inspection movement, not unrestricted first-person walking. Camera changes are immediate, so reduced-motion users do not receive forced travel animation. No character or video is implied. The new inspection does not record scores or evidence merely for clicking; camera orientation is local visual state.

## Asset and architecture

Original procedural GLB: `public/projects/shadow-gallery/coastal-3d-v1/workshop.glb`, generated by `docs/heist/build-coastal-workshop.mjs`. Hollow outer/inner hull geometry is joined at rim and ends; wooden rim, supports, stone implement, work stone and shavings are modeled. The asset contains 9 meshes and 26,768 triangles. Provenance and historical limitations are recorded alongside it. This is an illustrative reconstruction, not a measured historical replica.

Reusable `SpatialInspectionComponent` composes the prior GLTFLoader foundation. `SpatialInspectionDefinition` validates semantic target names, positions and camera distances. Optional `PanoramaViewpoint.inspection` chooses the renderer without a project-name branch. Spherical rendering is hidden and skipped during inspection; its prior position remains independent. No package changes or unrelated project edits.

Rendering occurs on camera changes rather than in a continuous loop. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5; one directional shadow is generated for the static scene. Controls, GLB resources, shadow targets, observers and renderer are disposed on exit. No runtime frame-rate result is claimed.
